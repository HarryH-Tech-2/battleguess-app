## Full description (4,000 chars max)
Fight your way through history.

BattleGuess turns the great battles of the world into a campaign you can play in five minutes a day. March from Marathon and Thermopylae through Hastings, Agincourt and Waterloo to D-Day and beyond, one lesson at a time.

EVERY BATTLE IS A LESSON
• 67 battles across 23 chapters, from the ancient world to the 20th century
• Each lesson opens with a painted scene and ends with the story of what happened
• Seven ways to answer: multiple choice, tap the map, slide the timeline, put events in order, match pairs, fill the blank and spot the lie

PLAY LIKE A GAME
• Hearts, combos and instant feedback keep every question tense
• Earn XP, climb the ranks and keep your daily streak alive
• Daily quests give you a reason to come back tomorrow
• A guide with a personality cheers you on and consoles you when you miss

BUILD YOUR CODEX
Every battle you master joins your Codex, a growing collection of painted battle cards with dates, places and outcomes.

MADE TO BE BEAUTIFUL
Original painted artwork for every battle, a campaign map that grows as you progress, and a dark ink-and-brass look that stays out of the way of the history.

No history degree required. Just curiosity and a few minutes a day.
