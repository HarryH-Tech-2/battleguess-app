Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 15, 2026

Summary: BattleGuess does not collect, transmit or share any personal data. The BattleGuess: History Quiz app for Android works entirely offline. Your progress and settings are stored only on your own device.

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy applies to the BattleGuess: History Quiz mobile application for Android (package name app.rork.battleguess.quiz, the "App") and to the website at www.battleguess.app (the "Website"). Together these are the "Service". The Service is developed and maintained by Harry Harrison, an independent developer ("we", "our", "us"). BattleGuess is not a company.

This policy explains what information the Service handles, how it is used, and the choices you have.

2. Information the App Stores on Your Device

The App does not ask you to create an account and does not collect personal information. To make the game work, it saves the following data locally on your device only:

  • Game progress: completed lessons and battles, XP, rank, hearts, streaks, daily quests and Codex entries.
  • Settings: your chosen language, theme, sound and vibration preferences, and your selected guide character.

This data never leaves your device. The App has no server of its own and makes no network requests. We cannot see, access or recover it.

3. Information the App Does Not Collect

The App does not collect or access:

  • Your name, email address, phone number or any other contact details
  • Your precise or approximate location
  • Your camera, microphone, photos, files or contacts
  • Device identifiers or advertising identifiers
  • Usage analytics, crash reports or diagnostics

The only Android permission the App declares is VIBRATE, which is used for optional haptic feedback and can be switched off in Settings.

4. Information Collected by the Website

When you visit the Website, our hosting provider (Vercel) may automatically record standard web server logs, which can include your IP address, browser type and version, pages visited and referring website. These logs are used only to keep the Website running securely and are retained according to the hosting provider's standard policies. The Website also loads fonts from Google Fonts, which may cause your browser to make requests to Google's servers; see Google Fonts privacy information for details.

5. How We Use Information

Data stored by the App is used solely to save your progress, remember your preferences and personalise your learning experience on your device. Website server logs are used solely to operate, secure and improve the Website.

6. Third-Party Services and Data Sharing

The App does not integrate any third-party analytics, advertising, social login or crash reporting services, and it does not share any data with third parties. We do not sell, trade or rent personal information to anyone.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

Data stored by the App remains on your device until you delete it. You can delete all of it at any time by clearing the App's storage in your device's Settings or by uninstalling the App. Because we hold no copy of your data, there is nothing for us to delete on our side and no deletion request is needed.

8. Children's Privacy

BattleGuess is an educational game suitable for general audiences. It does not collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us through the Website, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

9. Security

Because the App keeps all data on your device and transmits nothing, the security of that data depends on the security of your device. We take reasonable measures to protect the Website, but no method of transmission over the internet is completely secure.

10. Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or restrict the use of your personal data. Since the App stores no personal data with us, you can exercise these rights directly by managing or deleting the App's data on your device, as described in Section 7. For anything relating to the Website, contact us using the details below.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date. Significant changes will also be noted in the App's release notes on Google Play.
